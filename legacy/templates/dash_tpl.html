Sources: Carnegie Classifications 2025 Research Activity Designations public data file. Research spending is total R&D expenditure from the NSF HERD survey (FY2020–24 microdata), joined to IPEDS by UNITID plus a hand-checked crosswalk for HERD reporters with missing or outdated IDs. 516 of 541 institutions match Carnegie's FY2021–23 figures within 2%; the rest are flagged on their pages (combined campus reports, NSF revisions, or units that don't reconcile). Single-year swings over +60% or −40% are treated as likely reporting changes and left out of growth rates. Research doctorates are IPEDS Completions "Doctor's degree – research/scholarship," first major, AY2020–21 to 2024–25; AY2020–21 to 2022–23 reproduce Carnegie's counts for every designated institution exactly. AY2024–25 is IPEDS' first release and may be revised. The 2025 designations used the higher of the three-year average or the most recent year — the rule this tool applies to reproduce every 2025 designation. Private for-profit institutions are excluded (one: Ponce Health Sciences University, RCU). Carnegie has stated that future releases will use the three-year average only; the next update is scheduled for 2028. Cycle windows for 2028 and 2031 assume Carnegie keeps the 2025 data lag. Projections extend recent growth and are not forecasts. Prototype.
