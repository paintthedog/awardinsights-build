Sources: NSF award records FY2022–26 (data pulled Sept 19, 2026), joined to institutions by UEI and a hand-checked crosswalk. Coverage: of __NALL__ NSF awards worth __DALL__ made in this period, __NIN__ (__DIN__, __DPCT__ of dollars) are matched to one of the __N__ institutions here; the rest went mostly to non-university recipients (research consortia, federal labs, nonprofits, companies) or to private for-profit institutions, which are excluded throughout, so totals here are not NSF's totals. Dollars: by default each award counts at its full intended value in the fiscal year it was made, not the amount paid out so far; that keeps years comparable, since continuing grants are paid in annual increments, but it counts money that could still be cut. The "Obligated to date" switch shows instead what NSF has actually committed on each award so far, which understates recent years. FY2026 is a partial year. Ranks are competition ranks among the institution and its peers (T- marks a tie); no rank is shown where the institution has nothing in that row or the peer group has fewer than 4 members. Peers and filtered sets are drawn from the full Carnegie universe of __N__ degree-granting institutions whether or not they hold NSF awards. Institutional factors: 2025 Carnegie Classifications, IPEDS, NSF HERD. R&D bands use each institution's exact total R&D from HERD (FY2024, or FY2023 if that's its latest report); campuses HERD reports together use Carnegie's FY2023 campus figure. Texas A&M's engineering and agriculture agencies are counted with the College Station campus, as HERD reports them. An institution showing $0 may hold small awards not yet resolved in the crosswalk.
